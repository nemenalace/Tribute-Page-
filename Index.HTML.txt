      <header><b>Chadwick Boseman</b></header>
      <p id="tribute-info">Chadwick Boseman wrote his first play after a teammate of his was shot and killed. Giving him a passion for playwriting. </p>
      <p>He attended Howard University to study directing. During his time there, he took acting classes in the summer program at the University of Oxford.</p>
      <p>His first performance was "Rhyme Deferred" where he also co-wrote the screen play. His next project he directed and wrote again was named "Hieroglyphic Graffti" That debuted in the national black theatre festival in 2001.</p>
      <p>Around 2003. Boseman begun to appear on television with shows such as "Third Watch" as a guest appearence. His big films "The Express" and "42" playing as Jackie Robinson. With later films such as "Get on up" and his most iconic role in "Avengers: Civil war".      </p>
      <p>The final role that Boseman played was in the film titled "Ma Rainey’s Black Bottom" he was awarded an Acadaemy award posthumously </p>
